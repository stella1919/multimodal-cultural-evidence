# Research Questions

**RQ1.** What computable similarities and differences can be observed in visual forms and textual semantics across cultural contexts?

**RQ2.** To what extent are visual similarities aligned with or decoupled from semantic similarities?

**RQ3.** Can temporal, geographic, and cultural metadata reveal potential cross-regional associations and patterns of cultural evolution?

The pilot treats computational outputs as candidate patterns for humanistic interpretation, not historical proof.
